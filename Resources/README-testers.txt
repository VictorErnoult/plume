Plume — trial version

You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 15 or newer.

1. Drag Plume to the Applications folder, then open it.
2. macOS refuses to open it: that is normal, this trial version is not registered with
   Apple yet. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Plume was
   blocked", click "Open Anyway", then confirm. You only have to do this once.
3. Plume asks for two permissions (Microphone and Accessibility) and downloads its
   transcription model: about 600 MB, once. After that, everything stays on your Mac.
4. To dictate: Control + Shift (⌃⇧), speak, then ⌃⇧ again. The text is pasted wherever
   your cursor is. The feather icon in the menu bar opens the window.
