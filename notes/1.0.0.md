Plume 1.0: everything that was missing to dictate anywhere without thinking about it.

- **Voice commands**: "new line", "new paragraph", "bullet point", "scratch that", "press enter", in English and French.
- **One rule per app** (Applications page): Message or Casual style, Enter after the paste, typing instead of pasting.
- **Smart insertion**: spacing, capitalization and final period adapted to what surrounds the cursor.
- **Pause** that really closes the microphone, and **call detection** (Zoom, Meet, Teams…): the notch offers to record the meeting.
- **Local AI** (Apple Intelligence, off by default): polish, summary and title for meetings, rewriting a selection from a spoken instruction.
- **History**: titles, export to Markdown / text / SRT / WebVTT / JSON, transcribe again, how long audio is kept.
- **No more lost recordings**: a cancelled recording stays recoverable for 7 days (↶ button in History, menu or shortcut), and the cancel shortcut is configurable.
- **Home**: a "Dictate" button, the latest three transcriptions to copy in one click, and "What's new" at the top right.
- **English interface** by default, French in Settings › General.

The detail is in "What's new", in the app.
