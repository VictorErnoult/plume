First public release of Plume: voice dictation and meeting transcription for macOS, 100% local.

**Install**: download `Plume.dmg`, drag Plume into Applications, then open it. This version isn't notarized by Apple yet: the first time you open it, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **"Open Anyway"**. Later versions will install themselves.

Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or newer.
